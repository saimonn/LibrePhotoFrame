New features:
• Fill the frame from any synced folder: pick a local folder, turn on Watch Folder, and photos added or removed by Syncthing or any other sync tool show up on their own
• Choose the order of the next photos: random, capture time (EXIF), file creation time or file modification time
• Show two photos at once when they match the shape of the screen (Split Screen)
• Show a clock on the slideshow, with its size, position and 12/24-hour format
• Show the date and location of a photo, optionally resolving place names with OpenStreetMap and a handwritten script font
• Choose the app language (English, German, French, Spanish) and the screen orientation
• Pick an album of device photos and refresh it while the frame is running

Improvements:
• Automatic updates now also check when the app wakes up, so new versions arrive more reliably
• A photo is only shown once it stopped being written, so a file that is still being copied is never displayed half-written

Fixes:
• Photos a sync client adds to a device photo album now appear in the running slideshow without a restart
• The photo order, language and clock dropdowns no longer squeeze their row and make the settings unusable
• Settings are saved when the system back button closes the screen
• A paired photo is shown again after the frame changes shape, and a partner that no longer fits is dropped
• EXIF results are cached per file, so rescanning a large folder stays fast

Project:
• This app is now a fork of OpenPhotoFrame by Michael Wyraz, renamed LibrePhotoFrame
• The Contributor License Agreement was removed: contributions are simply published under the GPLv3, no signature is requested anymore
